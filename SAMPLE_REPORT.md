# Sample: Arabic E-commerce Customer Feedback Report

> **Illustrative sample only.** The comments and counts below are fictional examples created to demonstrate the report format. They are not real customer data or evidence of market demand.

## Example input

A hypothetical store provides 20 public customer comments about an order of household products.

## Example findings

| Theme | Example signal | What the store could investigate |
|---|---|---|
| Delivery delays | Several comments mention late arrival | Clarify delivery estimates and send tracking updates |
| Product quality | Comments mention items arriving damaged | Review packaging and supplier quality checks |
| Price objections | Customers say the price is high | Test bundles or explain product value |
| Product questions | Customers ask about sizes and availability | Make size and stock information easier to find |

## Example recommendations

1. Check whether delivery complaints cluster around a particular area or courier.
2. Compare damaged-item complaints with packaging type and product category.
3. Add clear size, availability, and delivery information to product pages.
4. Review the original comments before acting; keyword matches can miss sarcasm, negation, and context.

## What this report does not claim

- A comment asking about a product is not proof of a purchase.
- A repeated complaint is a signal to investigate, not proof of its root cause.
- These example findings are fictional and must not be presented as real customer research.
